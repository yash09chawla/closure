Research tools — accreditation & passing criteria

🔒 These two services take general, public information only (institution, degree, major, mode, duration, regulation year). Never enter a candidate's name, register number or date of birth. The server blocks requests that contain personal data, and every request sent to OpenAI is logged in data/audit/outbound.jsonl.

Accreditation (UGC / AICTE)

Searches the official UGC university lists and the AICTE 2025-26 approved-institution list. Produces an evidence PDF with browser screenshots (URL visible) and every source URL.

Passing criteria (official institute website)

Finds the regulation on the institute's own website only, highlights the passing clause and produces an evidence PDF (URL, screenshot, verbatim clause).
